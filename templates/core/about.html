About CodeIQ

Practical learning for ambitious developers

Last Updated: 26 July 2026

Who We Are

CodeIQ is an India-based online learning platform created to make programming knowledge and interview preparation more focused, accessible and useful. We bring structured learning materials into one place so learners can spend less time searching and more time understanding and practising.

What We Provide

  • free programming notes for accessible, self-paced learning;
  • premium PDF notes with deeper, structured coverage;
  • technology- and role-focused interview questions;
  • practical study materials for revision and preparation; and
  • video courses planned for future release.

All CodeIQ offerings are digital. Free resources and individually priced premium products allow learners to choose material that fits their needs without physical shipping.

Our Mission

Our mission is to turn scattered technical information into clear learning paths. We aim to create concise, practical resources that support students, early-career developers, working professionals and lifelong learners as they build confidence in technology.

How CodeIQ Works

Users sign in securely with Google, explore free or premium resources, and pay for selected premium products in Indian Rupees through Razorpay. Purchased digital content is ordinarily available after successful payment verification. We continually review the platform and plan to expand its formats as learners' needs evolve.

Our Principles

  • Clarity: well-organised material without unnecessary complexity.
  • Practical value: content designed for revision, understanding and preparation.
  • Accessible choice: useful free resources alongside focused premium products.
  • Responsible expectations: learning support without promises of jobs or interview outcomes.
