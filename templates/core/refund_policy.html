Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: 26 July 2026

Important: CodeIQ sells digital products only. A purchase generally cannot be cancelled or refunded after the content has been made accessible or downloaded.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to premium PDF notes, interview materials, study materials and future paid digital courses purchased from CodeIQ in INR through Razorpay.

2. Digital Products and General No-Refund Rule

Digital products are delivered or made available electronically. Because digital content cannot be physically returned, all sales are final and non-refundable after successful payment where access or a download has been provided. Please review the title, description, format, sample or preview, price and requirements before paying. A change of mind, accidental purchase, lack of use, or dissatisfaction based solely on personal preference does not qualify for a refund.

3. Cancellation

An order cannot ordinarily be cancelled after successful payment verification and digital fulfilment. If payment has not been completed, you may abandon the checkout without charge. Any mandatory remedy available under applicable Indian consumer law remains unaffected.

4. Duplicate Payments

If the same order is charged more than once, contact us with your registered email, order ID, Razorpay payment IDs, amount and payment proof. After verification, the duplicate amount will be refunded to the original payment method. Do not share an OTP, UPI PIN, CVV or banking password.

5. Failed or Pending Payments

If money is debited but CodeIQ shows the payment as failed or pending, first allow the bank or payment provider's normal reconciliation period. Failed transactions are often automatically reversed by the bank. If the amount is neither reversed nor the order confirmed after 5–7 business days, send us the transaction details. We will verify the status with Razorpay and either enable access after confirmed receipt or assist with the applicable reversal process.

6. Technical Issues

If successful payment is confirmed but the purchased file or access is unavailable, corrupted, or materially different from the product ordered, notify us promptly. We will first attempt to restore access, replace the file or provide a working download. A refund may be considered only when CodeIQ verifies the issue and cannot provide the purchased product within a reasonable period.

7. Refund Request Process

  1. Email support@yourdomain.com from your registered Google account email.
  2. Use the subject “Refund Request – [Order ID]”.
  3. Include your name, order ID, Razorpay payment ID, purchase date, amount, product title, reason and relevant screenshot.
  4. Submit the request within 7 days of the transaction or discovery of the delivery problem.

We may request additional information to verify the transaction. Eligible refunds are sent to the original payment method. Once initiated, the bank or payment provider controls posting time, which may typically take 5–10 business days. Processing timelines are estimates, not guarantees.

8. Chargebacks

Please contact us before initiating a chargeback so we can investigate. Fraudulent or abusive disputes may result in account restriction and submission of relevant order and access records to the payment provider.
