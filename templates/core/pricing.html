Pricing Policy

Last Updated: 26 July 2026

1. Pricing Model

CodeIQ offers free programming resources and individually priced premium digital products. The price shown on the relevant product page or at checkout is the price applicable to that purchase, subject to any obvious technical or typographical error. No physical products or shipping charges apply.

2. Currency

All prices are displayed and charged in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹), unless CodeIQ expressly states otherwise. If your payment provider uses another currency, it may apply its own conversion rate or fee, for which CodeIQ is not responsible.

3. Taxes

Applicable taxes, including GST where required, will be included in the displayed price or identified during checkout or on the invoice, as appropriate. The final payable amount is presented before you authorise payment. Tax treatment may change to comply with law.

4. Free Content

Resources marked “Free” may be accessed at no purchase price, subject to sign-in, licence and usage restrictions. Free content may be added, modified, moved or withdrawn without creating a right to compensation.

5. Premium Products and Payment

Premium PDFs, study materials and future courses are priced based on their scope, format and value. Payments are processed through Razorpay. Access is supplied after successful payment verification and remains governed by the product description and our Terms.

6. Promotional Discounts

CodeIQ may offer coupons, introductory prices, bundles or time-limited promotions. Promotions may have eligibility, product, account, redemption and expiry conditions; may not be combined; have no cash value; and may be withdrawn where misuse, fraud or technical error is suspected. A discount after your purchase does not entitle you to a retrospective refund.

7. Price Revisions and Errors

We may revise prices at any time due to content updates, operating costs, taxes, market conditions or business needs. A revision does not change a completed, successfully paid order. If an obvious pricing or system error affects an unfulfilled order, we may cancel it and refund the amount paid or invite you to purchase at the correct price.

8. Refunds and Contact

Digital-product refunds are governed by our Refund & Cancellation Policy. Pricing or invoice questions may be sent to support@yourdomain.com, CodeIQ, India, Monday–Saturday, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM IST.
