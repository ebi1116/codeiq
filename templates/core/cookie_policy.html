Cookie Policy

Last Updated: 26 July 2026

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser or device when you visit a website. Similar technologies such as local storage and pixels may perform related functions. This Policy explains their use on CodeIQ and should be read with our Privacy Policy.

2. Cookies We Use

  • Strictly necessary cookies: support sign-in, session continuity, CSRF protection, security, load management, checkout and digital delivery. The platform may not work correctly without them.
  • Preference storage: remembers choices such as visual theme or interface settings.
  • Analytics cookies: where used, help us understand visits, feature usage, performance and errors using aggregated or pseudonymous data.
  • Third-party cookies: Google Sign-In and Razorpay may set or read cookies during authentication or payment under their own policies.

3. Why We Use Cookies

We use these technologies to authenticate users, prevent fraud, keep accounts and payments secure, remember settings, provide purchased content, diagnose errors and improve user experience. CodeIQ does not use cookies to store your Google password, complete card number, UPI PIN or banking password.

4. Duration

Session cookies expire when the browser session ends. Persistent cookies or local-storage entries remain for a defined period or until deleted. Duration depends on their purpose, security requirements and the settings of the relevant third-party service.

5. Your Choices

You can delete or block cookies through browser settings. Where required, you may accept or reject non-essential cookies through a consent control. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent Google login, checkout, saved settings and downloads from functioning. You can also manage Google-related data in your Google Account settings.

6. Updates and Contact

We may update this Policy as technologies or legal requirements change. Questions can be sent to support@yourdomain.com, CodeIQ, India.
