Digital delivery

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: 26 July 2026

No physical shipping: CodeIQ sells only digital products. No item will be packed, couriered or delivered to a postal address, and no shipping fee applies.

1. Digital Products

CodeIQ provides free programming notes, premium PDF notes, interview questions, study materials and, in future, video courses. All such products are supplied electronically through the platform.

2. Payment Verification

Paid delivery begins after Razorpay reports a successful transaction and CodeIQ verifies the payment. A bank debit alone does not confirm an order where the gateway status remains pending or failed. Verification is usually automated but may be delayed by network, bank, risk-review or reconciliation issues.

3. Instant Digital Delivery

After successful payment verification, the purchased digital product is ordinarily made available instantly in your CodeIQ account, purchase library or on-screen confirmation page. Access is tied to the Google account used for the purchase. You are responsible for signing in with the correct account and maintaining access to it.

4. Download Availability

Downloadable PDFs may be accessed through your account as indicated on the product page. Download links are personal and must not be shared. Reasonable access limitations may be used to prevent fraud or redistribution. Future streaming courses may be view-only and subject to the access period disclosed at purchase.

5. Email Confirmation

Where enabled, an order confirmation or receipt is sent to the email address associated with your Google account. Email is supplementary; a delayed or filtered email does not mean delivery failed if the product appears in your account. Please check spam or promotions folders and confirm that your registered address is correct.

6. Delays and Delivery Support

If verified payment succeeds but access does not appear within a reasonable time, sign out and back in, check your purchases, and contact support@yourdomain.com. Include your registered email, order ID, Razorpay payment ID, product title, date, amount and screenshot. We will verify the transaction and restore delivery where appropriate.

7. No Shipping Address or Tracking

We do not request a delivery address for shipping, provide courier tracking, offer cash on delivery, or deliver outside the platform by post. Billing information may still be collected where needed for payment, invoices, tax or compliance.
