This Privacy Policy explains how CodeIQ ("CodeIQ", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, discloses and protects information when you visit or use our online learning platform, purchase digital products, or communicate with us. By using CodeIQ, you acknowledge the practices described here.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to CodeIQ's website, user accounts, free programming notes, premium PDF notes, interview questions, study materials and future digital courses. It does not govern third-party websites or services that maintain their own privacy policies.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use CodeIQ, we may collect:

  • Account information: name, email address, profile image and Google account identifier supplied through Google Sign-In.
  • Transaction information: order number, product purchased, amount, currency, payment status, Razorpay payment/order identifiers and invoice details. We do not directly store complete card, UPI PIN, banking password or similar payment credentials.
  • Usage information: pages viewed, searches, downloads, purchases, bookmarks, approximate location derived from IP address, browser, device type, operating system, referring URL, timestamps and diagnostic logs.
  • Communications: information included in support requests, refund requests or other messages you send us.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: session identifiers, preferences, security tokens and analytics data.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide, personalize and improve CodeIQ and its learning content;
  • authenticate users and maintain account security;
  • process orders, verify payments, issue invoices and provide purchased downloads;
  • respond to support, refund, legal and rights requests;
  • measure platform performance and understand product usage;
  • detect fraud, abuse, security incidents and violations of our Terms; and
  • comply with tax, accounting, legal and regulatory obligations.

Where applicable, processing is based on your consent, performance of our contract with you, compliance with law, or our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the platform.

4. Google Authentication

CodeIQ uses Google Sign-In as its account authentication method. When you choose to sign in, Google shares permitted profile information such as your name, email address, profile image and a unique account identifier. CodeIQ does not receive your Google password. Google's handling of your data is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy. You may review or revoke CodeIQ's access through your Google Account settings; revocation may prevent you from accessing account-only features.

5. Cookies and Analytics

We use essential cookies for sign-in, session continuity, security, payment flow and saved preferences. We may use analytics technologies to understand aggregate traffic, feature usage and errors. Where consent is required, non-essential analytics cookies will be used only after consent. Browser settings can block cookies, but disabling essential cookies may cause account, checkout or download features to stop working. See our Cookie Policy for details.

6. Payment Information and Razorpay

Paid transactions are processed through Razorpay. When you make a payment, payment and device information is transmitted directly to Razorpay and relevant banks, card networks or payment systems. CodeIQ receives limited transaction data needed to confirm payment, prevent fraud, fulfil your order, handle support and meet record-keeping requirements. Razorpay acts under its own privacy policy and security practices. Never send us your OTP, UPI PIN, CVV or banking password.

7. Third-Party Services and Disclosures

We may share the minimum necessary information with service providers that support authentication, payments, hosting, analytics, communications, security, accounting and legal compliance. We may also disclose information when required by law, court order or lawful government request; to protect users, CodeIQ or the public; in connection with a business restructuring; or with your direction or consent. We do not sell personal information.

8. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls, secure connections, restricted privileges and monitoring. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting access to your Google account and devices.

We retain personal data only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including maintaining purchased-content access, resolving disputes, preventing abuse and meeting legal, tax and accounting requirements. Transaction records may be retained for periods required by Indian law. Data is deleted or anonymised when no longer required, subject to backups and lawful retention obligations.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable Indian law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information; withdraw consent where processing depends on consent; raise a grievance; or request account closure. Some information may be retained where legally required or necessary to establish or defend legal claims. To exercise a right, email us from your registered address. We may verify your identity before acting on the request.

10. Children's Privacy

CodeIQ is not directed to children under 13. Users below 18 should use the platform only with the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian and must be legally capable of entering into the relevant transaction under Indian law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided information improperly should contact us for review and deletion.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology or law. The updated version will be posted here with a revised “Last Updated” date. Material changes may also be communicated through the platform or registered email address where appropriate.
